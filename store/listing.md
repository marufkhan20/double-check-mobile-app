## Full description (max 4000 chars)

Did I lock the front door? Did I turn off the stove?

DoubleCheck ends that doubt. It's a deliberately simple ritual you run on your
way out the door — so you leave with peace of mind instead of second-guessing
yourself all day.

HOLD TO CONFIRM
Tapping a box is too easy to do on autopilot. In DoubleCheck you press and hold
each item for a moment — a smooth ring fills and your phone gives a gentle
buzz the instant it's confirmed. That tiny pause is the whole point: it means
you actually looked.

EVERYTHING IN ITS PLACE
• Build your own checklist — Stove, Front Door, AC, Garage, anything.
• Each confirmed item is stamped with the exact time you checked it.
• A calm dashboard tells you "You're all set" when everything's done.
• See the last days you completed your routine at a glance.
• Reset with one tap for the next day.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account. No sign-up. No internet. Everything stays on your device — there's
no server to send your habits to, because there isn't one. DoubleCheck collects
nothing.

BUILT TO BE CALM
An ultra-minimal, OLED-friendly dark design with large, easy targets for when
you're rushing out the door. No clutter, no noise — just certainty.

Leave home knowing.
